## Measurements

Every case has 300 sampled frames (6.0 s at 50 Hz), so total = **2,100 frames**.
`time_to_first_1s_stable_s` is when the first *continuous* 1 s qualifying
window ends, **not** the instant the duck first becomes upright. In the push
case it is counted after the 1 s disturbance time.

| push | PASS | 1.72 after disturbance | 0.1154 | 0.8 | +0.2754 / -0.0048 |

**Interpretation:** Distinct initial roll180/pitch180 produce **different**
qpos paths (not duplicated results). Despite clearing the upright threshold,
some episodes wander 16–28 cm before stabilising. The acceptance gate does
not constrain horizontal displacement, path efficiency, energy, impact peak,
or landing/contact forces. The 0.7 m/s `push` is an instantaneous horizontal
velocity injection, not a calibrated force. Do not label these outcomes
"hardware success".

## Crucial fidelity limits

This pilot uses **official MJCF scene with robot-ground contacts** but the
approximate **position actuator model** (`PolicyInference(..., bam_ctrl=None)`)
instead of the BAM M6 voltage/friction model. The official v8 policy was trained
under backlash/domain randomisation, while this uses the non-backlash scene.
One deterministic trajectory per case is insufficient to estimate success
rates. An upright terminal posture in this simulator is a successful
**software smoke test**, not evidence of robust sim-to-real recovery.

Next physical gate: source-provenance check for BAM + backlash, 3+ distinct
seeds or more, multiple randomized body/joint spawns; report actual
`success_count/total`, displacement, impact/contact force and motor stress.
Future Android 3D viewer export must use **original MicroDuck geometry** and
check CC BY-NC-SA licence; do not publish a made-up surrogate geometry or
auto-merge EN01/PR #91 changes.
