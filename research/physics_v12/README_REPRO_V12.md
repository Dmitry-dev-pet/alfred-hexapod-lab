# CAD30 Freenove V11 — offline MuJoCo physics evidence (V12)

Date: 2026-11-06 · Issue #64 · This is NOT a hardware certification and does not change the Android viewer.

## Scope

53 unforced free-base `mj_step` tests of exact precomputed V11 controller joint targets: 29 nominal, 10 heavier body, 7 reduced tibia/floor friction, 7 reduced leg motor torque. Gravity, original six tibia collision meshes, contact normal forces and contact-point tangential speeds are measured. Absolute leg `q` targets are generated by the exact JS `PlantedCombinedPilot` used in V11. **Neither the viewer's ground-lock/root placement nor any foot welding is applied to the physics model.**

Detailed Russian findings: [`REPORT_V12.md`](REPORT_V12.md). Machine-readable comparison: [`SUMMARY_V12.json`](SUMMARY_V12.json). Full raw evidence and exact 29 command traces in the Drive archive.

## Local reproduction

1. Keep original private owner CAD30 at `source/mjcf/scene_local30_nominal.xml`, `source/mjcf/scene_local30_upper.xml` with `source/meshes/`, using original SHA-256 of nominal scene `fe9d7c292b199f19b11da220e74f826e05fb5433a43c5b46c1bc620eb34f6df3`. These **are not** part of the artifact and must remain in Drive/private storage.
2. Python 3.13, MuJoCo 3.14.0, NumPy 2.x, SciPy; Node 22.x. Run from the root of the Hexapod Lab worktree containing V7–V11 controllers and datasets.
3. Run the commands at the bottom of [`REPORT_V12.md`](REPORT_V12.md). Tests use raw JSON records and do not require a display.

The local serial tests repeat all 53 scenarios in under about a minute on a supported machine, but no runtime estimates or guarantees are made. No browser MuJoCo/Drake, no real motor commands, no on-device physics.

## Acceptance criteria and caveats

- **MODEL_STATIC_HOLD**: free-body simulation stays upright with load-bearing foot contacts, no actuator saturation and low tangential contact speed; isolated OEM standing yaw ±15° qualifies under nominal friction and assumed model parameters.
- **LOCOMOTION_NOT_VALIDATED**: no actual fall over the finite run, but stance-contact dropouts, contact motion/slip, torque saturation and mismatched commanded vs actual yaw/advance prevent declaring the gait physically correct. All OEM V11 gait scenarios have at least one such issue.
- **HARDWARE_UNVERIFIED**: real PWM/shaft-angle offsets, actuator torque-speed curves, surface friction, contact pads, battery voltage, collision slack and physical mass/COM are unmeasured. Do not use this evidence to command real servo limits or claim stable physical walking.
